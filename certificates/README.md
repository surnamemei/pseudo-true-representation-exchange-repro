About the records:
- **Unsuffixed copies.** `finite_width_epsilon_certificate.json` and `epsilon_interval_obligations.csv` are byte copies of their `_0p035` versions.
- **Numerical fields.** Candidate or reference files, such as `*_reference.json` and `stage14_continuum_candidate.json`, are floating or high-precision numerical inputs, not proof premises.
- **Old metadata.** `N31_global_certificate.json` keeps eight superseded comparison-metadata fields; see `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_README.md`, section 4.
- **Not here.** The 539 MB R6 intermediate `stage17_epsilon_inner_tiled_0p035.json` is regenerated by the replay. Its frozen hash is in `../replay/frozen_output_hashes.json`.
