# Lemma: compactification of two-tone fitted subspaces

Let N>=3 be odd and t range over N consecutive integers. Let T=R/(2*pi*Z) be the physical frequency circle. Use the compact symmetric product Sym^2(T)=(T x T)/(permutation), including its diagonal. For distinct nodes define

\[\mathcal S(\nu_1,\nu_2)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{e^{i\nu_1t},e^{i\nu_2t}\}.\]

On the diagonal define the confluent plane

\[\mathcal S(\nu,\nu)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{e^{i\nu t},t e^{i\nu t}\}.\]

The diagonal means the limiting rank-two plane, not the rank-one span obtained by duplicating an ordinary column.

In a local frequency lift write nu_1=m-h,nu_2=m+h and use the basis

\[c_{m,h}(t)=e^{imt}\cos(ht),\quad d_{m,h}(t)=e^{imt}t\operatorname{sinc}(ht).\]

For h!=0 its span equals the ordinary two-tone span. At h=0 it equals the stated confluent plane. The columns are independent at h=0 and remain so nearby; their Gram inverse and projector therefore depend continuously on (m,h). Away from the diagonal ordinary Vandermonde independence gives the same conclusion. Changing a lift by 2*pi leaves the physical sampled exponentials and projectors unchanged. At an antipodal pair either shortest-arc midpoint yields the same unordered plane. Thus the local extensions agree on overlaps and define a continuous projector on the compact quotient Sym^2(T).

Its image in the rank-two Grassmannian (or in finite-dimensional projector matrices) is compact. For each x, ||(I-P)x||^2 is continuous and attains its minimum there. Each confluent plane is a limit of ordinary planes, so this minimum equals the infimum over distinct ordinary two-tone fits. Rank-one fits are included as vectors in those planes and require no additional chart.

**Attainment qualification.** Compactification alone proves attainment in the closure. An ordinary two-tone minimum need not exist for an arbitrary x if the best plane is confluent. In the theorem, the strict coalescent margin forces the closure minimizer into separated A/B planes; only then is ordinary attainment established.
